What this design is not

✕Pub/sub Subject IDs

No Cyphal-style session manager or dynamic node allocation. Addressing is MAVLink sysid/compid.

✕CAN / UDP routing trait

Transports are RF radios; "routing" means choosing the healthier of two redundant RF channels — not subject/node dispatch.

✕Security as an add-on

Crypto and anti-jam sit in the main data path, not off to the side. They are the reason the protocol exists.
